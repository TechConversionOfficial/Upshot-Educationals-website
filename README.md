## The Challenge

Students researching language training and international education often arrive with very different questions. A learner preparing for IELTS may need coaching, mock tests and an exam study plan. Another learner may be comparing PTE and OET requirements. Someone aiming to study overseas may need to explore destinations, education options, admission documents, test requirements and the scope of counselling support.

A general-purpose education homepage can make these journeys harder if test preparation, overseas study guidance and enquiry actions compete for attention. A useful website should **explain what the institute offers, separate student goals, establish credible educational experience and make it easy to speak to the right counsellor**.

This challenge is inferred from the business's published services and the agency's case-study positioning. A dated usability or conversion audit of an earlier production website was not available.

## How the Website Project Is Positioned

The following priorities describe **the documented project category and appropriate user journeys**. They are not claims that every feature was tested or is currently deployed on the unreachable production site.

1. **Start with a clear student proposition.** Explain that Upshot Educationals supports language preparation and overseas-study decisions, instead of requiring visitors to interpret a generic education slogan.
2. **Separate the major examination journeys.** Distinguish IELTS, PTE and OET coaching so learners can identify the relevant preparation route without reading unrelated programme descriptions.
3. **Explain the support behind coaching.** Present programme format, trainer guidance, assessment approach, batch options and mock-test availability **only where approved current information exists**.
4. **Give study-abroad counselling its own pathway.** Help prospective international students understand the questions a consultation can address, such as course selection, admissions, eligibility and documentation.
5. **Make destination information useful, not promotional.** If the site covers countries such as Canada, Australia, the UK, the USA and New Zealand, keep entry requirements, visa rules and institution details current and appropriately qualified.
6. **Offer a straightforward counselling enquiry.** Encourage visitors to ask for batch details or request a consultation. Any contact form, callback, WhatsApp or booking workflow requires a live test before being called functional.
7. **Use verified trust signals.** Draw on real trainer profiles, approved testimonials and official partner listings, avoiding unauthorised university logos, unverified success rates and guaranteed visas or exam bands.
8. **Prioritise mobile readability.** Keep service navigation, examination details and enquiry prompts legible for students browsing on phones; a separate device-based performance test would still be needed.
9. **Use an editable website foundation.** A saved project record documents **custom WordPress website development**. That establishes the engagement type, but not the specific active theme, plugins or third-party integrations.
10. **Keep the language inclusive and practical.** Use plain instructions, short sections, descriptive calls to action and transparent counselling terms for students and parents comparing providers.

## Website Quality and Compliance Checks

These are **appropriate evaluation criteria**, not assertions that the client website has passed a completed audit.

- **[Core Web Vitals](https://web.dev/articles/vitals):** test page loading, responsiveness and visual stability on typical student devices.
- **[WCAG 2.2 Level AA](https://www.w3.org/TR/WCAG22/):** review keyboard navigation, descriptive labels, readable forms, contrast and meaningful image alternatives.
- **[Google Search Essentials](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/essentials):** maintain useful course descriptions, descriptive page titles and crawlable internal navigation.
- **[Schema.org](https://schema.org/):** assess accurate EducationalOrganization, Course, Service, Organisation and BreadcrumbList metadata where appropriate.
- **Exam and certification accuracy:** explain which exam the course prepares for, current formats, test-provider distinctions and the difference between coaching and official test administration.
- **Study-abroad guidance:** update destination policies, admission requirements, fees and visa-information disclaimers using authoritative sources; never guarantee admission, visas or migration outcomes.
- **Student privacy:** handle names, phone numbers, exam scores, passport details and application information under applicable data-protection and consent requirements.
- **Trust and attribution:** check staff titles, authorised partner logos, awards, testimonials and the actual scope of agency involvement before publishing them.
